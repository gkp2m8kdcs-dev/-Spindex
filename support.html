Frequently asked questions

How do I reset Spindex?

Open the app, go to Settings → Reset Custom Wheels to remove the wheels you have created (and any attached photos), or Settings → Clear Spin History to wipe only your spin log. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

How do I import my own photos into a wheel?

In the Editor, add a slice, then tap the photo placeholder to choose from your library or take a new one. The image is stored inside the app's private sandbox; nothing leaves your iPhone.

Does Spindex need the internet?

No. Spindex runs fully offline. There is no account, no server, and nothing to sync.

Does Spindex track me?

No. Spindex does not track you and does not use the IDFA or any equivalent cross-app identifier. See our Privacy Policy for the full App Tracking Transparency declaration.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Open the App Store → tap your profile icon → Purchase History → find Spindex → Report a Problem. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Where can I learn more about how the spin is computed?

Spindex uses a deterministic local random generator seeded per spin. This is enough for everyday choices — it is not cryptographically secure and should not be used for lotteries or any security-relevant purpose.
