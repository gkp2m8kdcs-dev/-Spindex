Spin with confidence.

Each wheel is rendered as a smooth, color-rich pie with a bouncing pointer. Tap Spin and the wheel rotates through a physically realistic deceleration, settling on a slice with a satisfying haptic, a glowing result sheet, share-ready text, and an automatic entry in your history.

For group decisions, switch to Multi-Pick and the app draws several winners one after another, without replacement.

App Tracking Transparency

Apple requires apps to disclose whether they track you. Here is the full declaration for Spindex.

● Tracking: Off

Does Spindex track you?

No. Spindex does not track you. Spindex does not collect, share, or sell any personal data, and it does not use the device's identifier for tracking (IDFA) or any equivalent cross-app identifier. The App Tracking Transparency prompt is therefore not shown because there is nothing to ask permission for.

Specifically, Spindex does not:

  • Use third-party analytics SDKs (Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • Use advertising or attribution networks.
  • Use crash-reporting or remote logging services.
  • Build an advertising or marketing identifier profile.
  • Combine user data across other apps or websites.

All data created inside Spindex — wheels, photos, history — is stored locally inside the app's private sandbox and is never transmitted off your device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Spindex does not use accounts, logins, or email addresses.

Does Spindex use my photos?

Only if you choose to attach one to a slice. Photos are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.

Will I see ads or in-app purchases?

No. Spindex is a one-time paid app with no ads, no trackers, and no upsells.
