Privacy Policy

Spindex is built to be private by default. This page explains exactly what that means in plain language.

1. Summary

Spindex does not require an account and does not connect to any remote server for everyday use. The wheels you create, the photos you import, your spin history, and your settings all live inside the app's local sandbox on your iPhone. Nothing about how you use Spindex is sent off your device, and nothing is shared with third parties.

We don't use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or crash-reporting services. There are no ads. The full, plain-language details are below.

● Tracking: Off

2. App Tracking Transparency Declaration

Spindex does not track you. For the purposes of Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, this means:

  • Spindex does not access the device's identifier for advertisers (IDFA) or any equivalent identifier.
  • Spindex does not link your activity in Spindex with data collected by other companies' apps or websites.
  • Spindex does not target advertising, perform ad attribution, or build an advertising profile.
  • Spindex does not share your data with data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Because there is nothing to ask permission for, Spindex does not display the “Ask App Not To Track” / “Allow Tracking” prompt. Your device's “Allow Apps to Request to Track” setting has no effect on Spindex, because Spindex never makes such a request.

The complete data-handling summary is also published in the App Store listing for Spindex under “App Privacy”.

3. What data Spindex stores

Spindex stores only what is needed to make the app work, and only on your device:

  • Wheels you create or edit: name, slice titles, colors, weights, and ordering.
  • Photos attached to slices: copied into the app's private storage (see Photos).
  • Spin history: timestamp, wheel name, and result for every spin you perform.
  • Settings: haptic on/off, default sort, and similar preferences.

This data is stored inside the app's container, in your iPhone's local file system, under your iOS user profile. It is not written to iCloud Drive, not synced, and not transmitted.

4. Photos you add

Spindex uses the standard iOS Photo Picker (PHPickerViewController) to attach photos to wheel slices. Photos are imported through the system picker, which runs in-process and never exposes the original photo library to the app.

Once imported, the image file is copied into the app's private Documents directory. It is not uploaded, shared, or made visible to other apps unless you explicitly tap Share on a result screen — in which case iOS's standard share sheet is shown and you decide where, if anywhere, the content goes.

You may revoke Spindex's access to your photo library at any time in Settings → Privacy → Photos → Spindex on iOS. Revoking access does not delete photos already imported into the app; to remove those, delete the slice in the Editor or reset custom wheels.

5. Analytics, ads, and third parties

Spindex contains:

  • No analytics SDK (no Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Branch, or similar).
  • No advertising SDK and no ad mediation.
  • No crash-reporting or remote-logging SDK (no Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag, or similar).
  • No marketing automation, push, or in-app messaging SDK.
  • No social-login or account SDK.

No third party receives data from Spindex. Spindex does not contain tracking pixels, webviews, or remote content loads of any kind.

6. Share & system features

When you tap Share on a spin result, iOS shows its standard share sheet. Anything you send through that sheet goes directly from your device to the app or person you choose, governed by that destination's own privacy practices. Spindex does not see or log the destination.

Spindex uses Apple's UIActivityViewController only; we never call third-party sharing APIs.

7. Deleting your data

You can wipe local data at any time, without uninstalling:

  • Settings → Clear Spin History removes every recorded spin.
  • Settings → Reset Custom Wheels removes wheels you have created, including any photos attached, and restores the built-in library.
  • Uninstalling Spindex removes everything the app has stored on your iPhone, including photos and history.

Because Spindex does not store data on any server, there is nothing else to delete.

8. Children's privacy

Spindex is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age defined by your local law).

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how data is handled, we will update this page in the next app release so you can review the differences. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.

Material changes will also be highlighted in the App Store “What's New” notes for Spindex.
